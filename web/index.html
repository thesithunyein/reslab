How it works

The honest path, step by step

What does ResLab do?

ResLab is a lab notebook for research that wants to stay honest. You design a study, seal the plan with a SHA-256 checksum before you see the data, run verified statistics, and keep every action on a tamper-evident audit trail.

Why pre-register?

When the plan is locked before data collection, a result that is not significant is still a result. The checksum binds the plan, and the integrity detectors catch you if the analysis drifts from it.

What tests are included?

Welch and Student t-tests, one-way ANOVA, Mann-Whitney U, and correlation. The engine checks normality and variance assumptions first and picks the test the data actually supports.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Analysis runs entirely in your browser, on your machine. Your CSV never leaves it.

What is the audit trail?

Every action is appended to a hash chain. Each entry stores the hash of the entry before it, so a silent edit breaks the chain and verification fails visibly.

How do I start?

Drop a CSV into the analyzer to get a verified result in seconds, or open the pre-registration workspace to lock a plan first.

Every number is computed, verified, and reproducible.
